Career & Background

Who I Am

The full story — where I have been, what I have built, and what drives the work.

"I have spent 25 years doing one thing well: building the channel infrastructure that turns a manufacturer's product into national revenue."

I started in Memphis in 1993, running my own furniture operation from a retail showroom. I learned to sell, learned to manage people, and learned what dealers and institutional buyers actually need from a vendor relationship. Those early years built the foundation for everything that came after.

When Compatico recruited me in 2007, I took on national sales responsibility for a 40+ rep network producing $12–15M annually. I spent 11 years there building the systems, the coaching programs, and the dealer relationships that made that network perform. I also directed the Giza 2020/CAP design software program — reducing proposal time for dealers by 40% and increasing bid conversions by 25%. That experience taught me that the best channel leaders are also builders: they do not just manage what exists, they create the tools and infrastructure that make their networks more effective.

After Compatico, I took on the VP of Sales role at Tremain, a Mexico-based office furniture manufacturer with zero U.S. presence. I built their national rep network from scratch — 30+ partners across 42 states in 18 months — and generated $7–10M in revenue over three years. That engagement became the template for what Southern Solutions Consulting does today.

Since then, SSC has placed me inside organizations at the VP and Director level to build what they need: rep networks, dealer programs, pricing infrastructure, sales team training. Every engagement is different. The approach is the same.
